# 🎵 LumiListen — Mood-Based Music Discovery Website

### *Feel it. Find it. Play it.*

LumiListen is an interactive music discovery website designed to help users discover music that matches their emotions and personal music preferences. Users can select their favourite music genres, choose how they feel, and explore personalised playlists inspired by their current mood.

The project combines music discovery, emotional expression, and an immersive visual experience through a modern, responsive, and user-friendly interface.

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Personalised Music Taste:** Choose from 12 music genres, including R&B, Amapiano, Hip-hop, Pop, Gospel, Afro-soul, House, Jazz, Rock, Classical, Lo-fi, and Electronic.
- 🎭 **Mood Selection:** Explore 10 different moods: Happy, Sad, Calm, Energetic, Angry, Anxious or Overwhelmed, Romantic, Nostalgic, Motivated, and Numb or Unsure.
- 🎧 **Mood-Based Recommendations:** Discover playlists and music suggestions inspired by your selected mood and music preferences.
- 🔍 **Discover Page:** Explore featured moods, popular genres, and trending-style playlists.
- 💜 **Immersive User Interface:** Experience cinematic photography, a dark theme, soft lavender accents, and music-inspired visuals.
- 🧭 **Interactive Navigation:** Navigate between the homepage, music preferences, mood selection, recommendations, music player, Discover, About, and How It Works pages.
- 🎶 **Interactive Music Player:** Explore track information, playback controls, favourites, playlists, and the queue through a simulated music player.

## 🖥️ Website Experience

LumiListen follows a simple, interactive journey:

1. **Homepage:** Discover LumiListen and begin your music discovery journey.
2. **Music Taste:** Select your favourite genres or choose **Surprise Me** to explore something unexpected.
3. **Mood Selection:** Choose one of the ten moods that best describes how you feel.
4. **Personalised Recommendations:** Explore mood-inspired playlists and suggested tracks.
5. **Music Player:** Interact with the simulated player and explore the track queue.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **React** — Builds reusable, interactive user interface components.
- **TypeScript** — Adds type safety to application logic and data structures.
- **CSS** — Creates the website's layouts, styling, animations, and responsive design.
- **Unsplash** — Provides photographic imagery for music genres, moods, and playlists.
- **React Hooks** — Manages application state and optimises derived data.

## 🎵 How It Works

LumiListen connects users' music preferences with their current emotions to create a more personal music discovery experience.

For example, a user feeling calm may receive peaceful playlist suggestions, while someone feeling motivated may discover energetic and inspiring music. The goal is to make discovering music more personal, engaging, and emotionally meaningful.

The interface combines expressive typography, music-inspired imagery, clear navigation, and responsive layouts to support an enjoyable experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🔮 Future Improvements

Users will be able to listen to real songs by real artists, discover new releases, explore artist profiles, and enjoy playlists tailored to their moods and music preferences. The platform will integrate with a suitable music streaming API or licensed audio provider to support real audio playback, play/pause controls, track skipping, playback progress, and personalised music recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of LumiListen is to demonstrate how interactive web development and thoughtful UI/UX design can create a personalised digital experience centred on music and emotions.

This project also provides an opportunity to practise component-based development, state management, TypeScript, responsive design, interaction design, and user-focused problem-solving.
