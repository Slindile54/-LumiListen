🎵 LumiListen — Mood-Based Music Discovery Website

Feel it. Find it. Play it.

LumiListen is an interactive music discovery website designed to help users discover music that matches their emotions and personal music preferences. Users can select their favourite music genres, choose how they feel, and explore personalised playlists inspired by their current mood.

The project combines music discovery, emotional expression, and an immersive visual experience through a modern, responsive web interface.

✨ Features
🎨 Personalised Music Taste: Choose from 12 music genres, including R&B, Amapiano, Hip-hop, Pop, Gospel, Afro-soul, House, Jazz, Rock, Classical, Lo-fi, and Electronic.
🎭 Mood Selection: Explore 10 moods, including Happy, Sad, Calm, Energetic, Angry, Romantic, Nostalgic, Motivated, Anxious or Overwhelmed, and Numb or Unsure.
🔍 Discover Page: Explore featured moods, trending-style playlists, and popular genres. 
💜 Immersive User Interface: Enjoy cinematic background photography, dark styling, lavender accents, and music-inspired visuals. 
🧭 Interactive Navigation: Move between the homepage, music preferences, mood selection, recommendations, player, Discover, About, and How It Works pages.  

💻 Website Preview
The LumiListen experience follows this journey:   
Homepage: Introduces LumiListen and invites users to begin their music discovery journey.  
Music Taste: Users select their favourite genres or choose "Surprise Me". 
Mood Selection: Users select one of ten moods. 
Recommendations: The website presents a mood-inspired playlist and music suggestions.  
Music Player: Users interact with a simulated player and explore the track queue.   

🛠️ Technologies Used
React — Building reusable and interactive user interface components.
TypeScript — Adding type safety to application logic and data structures.
CSS — Styling the website, layouts, animations, and responsive interface.
Unsplash — Providing photographic imagery for music genres, moods, and playlists.
React Hooks — Managing application state and memoising derived track data.  

🎵 How It Works
LumiListen connects user-selected music genres with their chosen mood to create a more personal discovery experience. For example, someone feeling calm may receive peaceful playlist suggestions, while someone feeling motivated may see energetic and inspiring recommendations.

🔮 Future Improvements
Upgrade LumiListen from a simulated music player into a fully functional music discovery and streaming platform. Users will be able to listen to real songs by real artists, discover new releases, explore artist profiles, and enjoy playlists tailored to their moods and music preferences. The platform will integrate with a suitable music streaming API or licensed audio provider to support real audio playback, play/pause controls, track skipping, playback progress, and personalised music recommendations.

🎯 Project Objective
The objective of LumiListen is to demonstrate how interactive web development and thoughtful UI/UX design can create a personalised digital experience centred on music and emotions.   This project also provides an opportunity to practise component-based development, state management, TypeScript, responsive design, interaction design, and user-focused problem-solving.
